## Description
RustChain's contributor guide uses a simple courtesy claim to reduce duplicate bounty work: comment `/claim`, get a seven-day visible claim, and renew it if needed. The important detail is that the claim does **not** reserve payment.

This short is based only on the public contributor guide:
https://github.com/Scottcjn/rustchain-bounties/blob/main/docs/HOW_TO_SUBMIT_A_BOUNTY.md
